HUD: the ten options (261026)

Mobile portrait, zero scroll. These change only what is drawn: the match, the brains and the physics are untouched. One exception: the hit label (#3) also changes in the browser viewer (Web/app.js).

What changed

#OptionDone as
1Momentum bar under the panelHangs off the panel's measured bottom. The tale of the tape, the replay line and the judges' slide-in card follow it, so the empty band over the crowd is gone.
2Bottom-centre menu gripA 120×44 grip between the BL and BR corners opens or closes the sheet. TR stays the spec's menu button.
3Hit label"Clean palm" / "Clean hit" at or above 8 m/s (the L2 line), otherwise "Palm" / "Hit". Every hit still scores. Same rule in Unreal and on the web.
4Win/loss cardA Slate card while the result holds: KNOCKOUT or DECISION, the result, judges, series, plus REMATCH and MENU buttons. It replaces the canvas banner.
5Live telemetry + replay in one placeAn x1/4 badge beside REPLAY on the MATCH tab sets the replay pace (1/2 or 1/4). The view badges already sat on that tab.
6Slim bottom panelsHEAD G and STAMINA/NECK are now 48 px strips (were about 100 px), so the feet stay visible.
7Gauge without the wordThe "PALM" label is gone: just the number and the 8 m/s tick. ABOUT calls it GAUGE.
8Shorter scoreboard3 lines: R1/3 BLUE CLOCK 28.4s, the touch, and J 0:0 0:0 0:0 SERIES 0-0. Was 6 lines.
9Notch / nav-bar test-SlapSafeZone=<top>+<bottom> fakes the insets. -SlapLayoutTest now also fails any corner anchor or the grip that sits outside them. The Slate layer pads from the same insets the canvas reads each frame, because SSafeZone kept a stale margin in the editor binary.
10GesturesOn the arena, a sideways swipe steps through the presets (none → TEL → TEL+SCI → XRAY) and a double tap replays.
