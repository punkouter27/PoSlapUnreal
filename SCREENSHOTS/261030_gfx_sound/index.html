Two bugs found on the way. (1) Wrapped light fed into the GGX specular made 152 pixels reach about 23,000 in linear HDR (it divides by ~0 at grazing angles), and bloom spread them over the whole frame: the wrap now feeds only the diffuse term. (2) three.js's physical material alone cost about 15 fps on the two athletes, so the skin is the standard material with the same hooks.
Frame rate at 540×960 in headless Chrome on the training machine (the GPU shared with a training run, so ±2× noise): before 56-60 fps; after, Ultra 16-72 fps and Low 42-52 fps. On a free GPU, Ultra held 60 fps (vsync).
