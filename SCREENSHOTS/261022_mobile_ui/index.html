Mobile portrait UI: corner anchors, safe zone, menu sheet (2026-10-22)

Before: the broadcast panels started at the very top edge, the build stamp sat alone bottom-right, and there was no menu or FPS readout. After: a Slate layer inside the device safe zone puts the title top-left, live FPS top-centre (green / amber / red), MENU top-right, a debug line bottom-left (match, round, camera shot, worst frame) and the build stamp bottom-right. The Canvas panels now start below the top bar and end above the bottom bar. Nothing scrolls.

Menu: tabbed bottom sheet

MENU opens a sheet over a dimmed scrim. The tabs are MATCH (round, striker, tale of the tape and brain per corner, series, last result), VIEW (show or hide telemetry, commentary and the debug line) and ABOUT (what the overlays mean, build). Tapping the scrim or X closes it. The match keeps running underneath.

Aspect ratios and safe zone

The camera holds the picture at 9:16, so the Canvas panels never clip. Taller phones get a letterbox and landscape screens get a pillarbox. The corner anchors follow the screen's own corners. Safe zone: simulated with r.DebugSafeZone.TitleRatio=0.85. The engine shrinks the Canvas into the title-safe box, and the Slate layer uses the same title-safe inset.
