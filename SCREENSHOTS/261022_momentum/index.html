Momentum bar (2026-10-22)

After: a red/blue bar under the match panel. Each brain's critic (its own expected reward over the next ~2 s) is compared with what that brain usually expects in its current role (an 8 s running average and spread per role, since a guard expects thousands and a striker about nothing); the bar is the logistic of the difference, smoothed. It shows who is doing better than usual right now, not a calibrated win probability: here red has just been knocked down (80 % blue) although red wins the match on cards.
