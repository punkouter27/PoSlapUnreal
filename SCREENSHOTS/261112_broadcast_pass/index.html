What to look at

  • The jumbotron picture is greyer than the main view. It is a tone-mapped capture shown on an unlit panel.
  • Soft tissue cannot be judged from stills. Watch the belly and jowl on a strike in motion, or in a reel.
  • The names on the mat are mostly hidden behind the fighters in the close walk-in shot; they read best from the intro crane.
  • The win probability model was fitted on 59 matches. Treat the number as rough until more real matches are logged.
