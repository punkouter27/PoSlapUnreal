2KO danger meter from neck damagedoneKO danger = 1 − (1 − balance)(1 − 0.6 × neck damage). Balance is the capture point outside the soles (local) or the last head snap (live). Neck damage = (1 − neck strength) / (1 − 0.5), the fatigue model's own number. The bar shows the banked neck part (dark red) under the total. It also drives the crowd's tension. In the TAB stats.
3FLINCH tagdonebroadcast.js Flinch: the defender's head moves away from the palm faster than 0.15 m/s for 30 ms while a swing is on (palm > 2.5 m/s), before contact. It goes into the defender's cue row, which already holds the AI read: FLINCH overrides READ for 2.2 s. It ties into the FEINT/REAL tag: a feint that drew a flinch reads FEINT x.x FLINCHED, and a slap on a flinched defender reads on a flinch (Blueprint L4's feint bonus). One flinch per swing, and a feint re-arms it. The threshold was measured on the shipped brains (below).
4Per-strike instant replaydoneEach step's qpos goes into a 1.28 s ring buffer. 0.2 s after a landed strike, the window from 0.3 s before the touch to 0.2 s after plays at 0.45× (~1.1 s) into a second MjData (mj_kinematics only) on the side shot. The caption shows palm speed and head snap. The match keeps stepping underneath on its own MjData. A tap, Esc or a tap on the caption skips it. The View tab has Replays on/off, and ?replay=0 turns it off. There was no KO replay in the browser to reuse (that one is Unreal's), so this is new. The flinch threshold

One match per pairing in Node, sampled each brain step after the turn's first second, before contact, as head velocity along the palm→head line:

pairingno swing on: p99 / maxswing on: p90 / max
gg0.031 / 0.033 m/s0.003 / 0.007 m/s (Grandpa's L4 brain does not flinch)
gk0.102 / 0.1210.389 / 0.537
kk0.139 / 0.163−0.078 / −0.069
kg0.101 / 0.1140.387 / 0.546

Kim's resting head drifts up to 16 cm/s, so a threshold of "a few cm/s" would tag Kim at rest. 0.15 m/s with the swing gate sits between resting drift and flinching. It is one constant, FLINCH_SPEED, in broadcast.js.
