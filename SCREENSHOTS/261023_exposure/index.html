Arena exposure and haze (2026-10-23)

Before: athletes washed out. Auto exposure metered the whole frame, mostly the dark crowd, so it pushed the brightly lit athletes into overexposure, and a thick haze plus strong beam scattering laid a milky veil over the stage. After: exposure is metered on the brightest quarter of the frame and set one stop lower (bias -2.5), the haze is halved (fog 0.0015), the beams are subtler, the key light goes 4500 to 3000 lm and the truss spots 1500 to 1000 lm, bloom is lower and contrast slightly higher. The athletes' skin and clothes keep their colour and the red mat is saturated again.
