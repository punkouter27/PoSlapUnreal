Tier 1Quick Look: Spatial Arena & Collision Philosophy

  • Arena Topology: Symmetrical rectangular stage centered at the world origin $(0, 0, 0)$ cm, facing along the Unreal $X$-axis.
  • Athlete Placement: Red Athlete stands at $X = -42.5$ cm facing $+X$; Blue Athlete stands at $X = +42.5$ cm facing $-X$. Total pelvis-to-pelvis separation is exactly $85.0$ cm ($0.85$ m).

Tier 3Setup Guide: Unreal vs. MuJoCo Collision Matrix

  • Chaos Channel Inactivation: In Unreal Engine, every channel on every placed mesh component is set to Ignore. This prevents Unreal's Chaos physics solver from applying conflicting forces, jittering transforms, or allocating ragdoll collision memories.
  • MuJoCo Dynamic Collision Solver: All contacts are solved natively with frictional cone dimensions of 3 (condim=3). Soles rest on the floor with friction 0.90; body parts interact with friction 0.80.
