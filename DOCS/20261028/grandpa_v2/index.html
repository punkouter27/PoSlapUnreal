Phase 2: rig and skinning, what changed

  • Root bone added at (0,0,0) with an identity rest; pelvis hangs from it. The armature object is named Armature so UE's FBX import doesn't add an extra root.
  • Mirrored joint axes. The scan rig's left and right bone rolls differed by up to 112° (feet) and 23° (hands). Mirrored axes stop IK solves from flipping and let animations mirror cleanly.
  • Skeleton vs simulator: joint positions match MuJoCo to 7.44 mm RMS (worst 20.37 mm, head).
  • Twist helpers were tested and dropped. I added shoulder, hip and wrist twist bones, then measured every variant over the full range of motion (lower score is better): original weights 1.0104, twist 1.0191, twist + smoothing 1.0688. Under the 4-influence mobile cap, the twist share has to come out of a vertex's existing slots, so it doesn't pay on this mesh. The pipeline kept original and removed the idle bones. With an 8-influence budget (desktop), twist bones would be worth another look.
  • Morph targets: Breathe (chest and belly along the normals, up to 1 cm) and Jiggle_Belly (a 1.2 cm sag). Same vertex count and order as the base mesh.
  • Weights: 2,678 / 3,629 / 1,127 / 2,550 vertices with 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 bones.

Phase 3: real-time hygiene

  • Geometry: 20,000 triangles, clean. LODs: LOD1 10,000 tris · LOD2 5,000 tris.
  • UVs: UV0 is the scan's own atlas (no overlap, uniform density). UV1 UVLightmap is new, padded, with no overlap.
  • Textures, 2048², in Content/Art/Exports/Grandpa_v2_Textures/: BaseColor (sRGB), Normal (OpenGL) + Normal_DX (UE green), ORM (R AO from a Cycles bake, G roughness, B metallic), Mask (HDRP: R metallic, G AO, B detail, A smoothness). Mean AO is 0.712.
  • Collision: 17 convex hulls named UCX_SM_Grandpa_v2_Proxy_## around a static proxy, in Grandpa_v2_Collision.fbx.

What this pass could not do (honest list)

  • Quad topology: the scan is a decimated triangle soup (0 quads). Clean quad edge loops at the shoulders and knees need a retopology pass (Quad Remesher or by hand). No script can produce good loops here.
  • UCX collision on a skeletal mesh: UE ignores UCX_ for skeletal meshes, and in this game MuJoCo's capsules are the physics anyway. The hulls are for a static proxy only.
  • Lightmap UV on a skinned mesh: skinned meshes aren't lightmapped. UV1 is there for the static proxy and for tools that want it.
  • Engine look matching (Lumen/VSM, HDRP): not run. It needs the asset imported into the editor first, after you approve.
  • Twist bones at runtime: they were dropped (see above), so no AnimBP or Control Rig is needed.

Phase 1: ten creative ideas (for your approval)

#AreaIdea
1Micro-detailEdge wear on the shirt collar, cuffs and trouser hems
A curvature mask (pointiness) multiplied into roughness and a slight desaturation. It reads as fabric that has been worn, and it costs one texture channel.
2Micro-detailSweat sheen that tracks fatigue
Roughness drops on the forehead and neck as stamina falls (the stamina signal already reaches the HUD). It makes tiredness visible without any text.
3Micro-detailSkin pores and age spots in a detail normal
A tiling 512 detail normal masked to skin by the base colour hue. The scan is soft at close-up range, and this restores it at no unique-texture cost.
4Micro-detailChalk dust on the palms and podium edge
A decal plus a palm-print mask (the wear shader already has PrintU/PrintV). It grounds every strike in the scene.
5LightingKey:fill 4:1 with a cool rim (used in these renders)
A warm key from three-quarter front, a cool fill, and a strong rim that separates the bald head from the dark crowd.
6LightingA tight spot on the podium with a gentle falloff
Focal contrast: the eye goes to the two heads and the podium, and the crowd falls into shadow.
7AtmosphereThin height fog plus a god ray through the truss
Adds depth on a phone screen, where small details vanish. Height fog with a volumetric scattering intensity of about 0.3 stays cheap.
8Scale and groundingContact shadows under the soles plus a scuffed footbox decal
Feet that float are the most common thing that makes a character read as fake. Capsule shadows in UE come straight from the physics shapes.
9Scale and groundingBevel or round the podium's top edges
A 1 cm bevel catches a highlight line, and the podium stops looking like an untextured box.
10CompositionVary the silhouettes and dress the background
Corner stools, a towel and a water bottle at each corner, and a referee figure. These break the symmetric two-figure silhouette and make the space feel lived in.
